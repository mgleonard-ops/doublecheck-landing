Why parents don’t tell you

Your dad has managed money for fifty years. He is not going to call you at work to ask whether a text from “his bank” is real. Asking feels like admitting he can’t cope on his own. And he worries that one question will turn into you second-guessing everything he does with his money.

So most people say nothing. FINRA’s research on elder financial exploitation estimates that for every dollar of reported losses, about $24 goes unreported. Embarrassment keeps it quiet. So does the fear of losing independence.

Double Check gives your parent a private way to ask. They paste the suspicious message into the app and get a calm, plain-English verdict in seconds. Nobody has to know they checked. No call to you, no feeling foolish. They stay independent, and they stop having to guess right every single time.

The 60-second setup

Do this the next time you’re together. It fits inside one cup of coffee.
