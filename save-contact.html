How to save it on your phone

Add on iPhone
  1. Tap the blue “Save this contact” button above.
  2. A contact card opens on your screen. Tap “Create New Contact” (you may need to scroll down to see it).
  3. Tap “Done” in the top-right corner. That's it — Double Check is now in your contacts.
Add on Android
  1. Tap the blue “Save this contact” button above. A small file called doublecheck.vcf downloads.
  2. Tap the download notification at the top of your screen (or open your Files app and look in Downloads).
  3. Your phone asks where to save it — choose Contacts and tap Save. Done.
