FAQ

Common questions

Honest answers about what Double Check does and how it works.

Is it free?
Yes. Free and unlimited checks, no credit card required. An optional Pro upgrade adds step-by-step guidance and family alerts for people who want extra support.
Do I need to create an account?
No account, no sign-up, no password. Open the app and start immediately. If you want to save your full check history or set up family alerts (part of Double Check Pro), you can optionally create an account, but it’s not required to use the core features.
Does it work on Android?
Yes. Double Check is live on both the App Store (iOS) and Google Play (Android). Tap the Google Play or App Store button at the top of this page to download.
What does the AI do with my message?
Your message is analyzed, a verdict is returned to you, and then the content is discarded. We never write the full content of what you submit to our servers. Nothing you paste into Double Check is retained or used for AI training.
What is the family alert?
Family alerts are part of Double Check Pro. You add a trusted email address in settings: a family member, an adult child, or a financial advisor. When a scam is detected, Double Check automatically sends that person an email. No extra steps required from you, and they don’t need the app. This keeps the people who care about you informed without anyone having to check in.
What kinds of scams does it catch?
Text messages, emails, suspicious URLs, phone call scripts, and screenshots. Common patterns include Medicare fraud, fake IRS notices, bank impersonation, romance scams, tech support scams, and fake package delivery alerts. If you can paste it or photograph it, Double Check can analyze it.
How accurate is it?
The AI catches the vast majority of common scam patterns. It is not perfect (no AI is) and we’re honest about that. The “What it can’t do” section above explains the limits clearly. Always verify with your bank or a trusted family member before sending money or sharing personal information.
How do I turn on Text Protection for my mom?
On her iPhone, with the Double Check app installed and up to date: 1. Open the Settings app. 2. Scroll down and tap Apps, then Messages. 3. Tap Unknown & Junk. 4. Under SMS Filtering, choose Double Check. That’s it: likely scam texts are moved to her Junk folder automatically. To be clear about the limits: it filters SMS text messages from senders who aren’t in her contacts. Messages from people she knows are never touched, and it doesn’t cover iMessages from known contacts, emails, or phone calls.
Who sees my family alerts?
Only the email address you enter as a trusted contact. You control who is notified. Nothing is shared publicly or with Double Check. If you don’t set up a family alert, no one else is notified when you run a check.
